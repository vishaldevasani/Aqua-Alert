About the Project

Why Flood Prediction & Water Sustainability Matter

Urban flooding is among the most destructive natural hazards of our era. As climate change intensifies rainfall events and cities expand onto flood-prone land, the gap between risk and preparedness grows wider every year. Globally, floods displace tens of millions of people annually and cost trillions in economic damage.

At the same time, water scarcity threatens over 2 billion people — a number set to rise sharply under continued warming. The paradox of simultaneous flood and drought risk, sometimes in the same city across different seasons, demands intelligent, data-driven tools accessible to everyone.

AquaAlert AI was built to bridge that gap. By combining live meteorological data with transparent risk logic and AI-generated sustainability guidance, it empowers communities, students, urban planners, and policymakers to understand and respond to environmental risks in real time — no specialised training required.

🌊

Flood Displacement

Over 20 million people are displaced by floods globally each year — more than any other natural hazard.

💧

Water Access Crisis

2.2 billion people lack safely managed drinking water services. Climate change is accelerating shortfalls.

🤖

AI for Good

AI-powered early warning systems can reduce disaster-related deaths and losses by up to 30%, per UN data.

🏙️

Urban Vulnerability

By 2050, 68% of the world's population will live in cities — making urban resilience a defining challenge.
